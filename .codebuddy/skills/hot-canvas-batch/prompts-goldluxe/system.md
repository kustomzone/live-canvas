# Flipbook Canvas — System Style Constraints (鎏金奢华)

All canvases share these constraints. This is a **抖音图文 (Douyin photo-text)** deck — every image must be a scroll-stopping vertical poster with black-gold luxury / high-fashion aesthetics.

- **Format**: vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), mobile-first, single coherent luxe scene.
- **Visual style**: black-gold luxury / premium editorial look — deep matte black backdrop, gleaming gold-foil metallic accents, fine gold linework, marble textures, soft spotlight on a single hero object, high-fashion magazine elegance. Think luxury brand campaign / 高级感 杂志 cover — refined, glossy, expensive.
- **Background**: deep black (#0B0B0B) or charcoal with subtle marble veining and a soft warm spotlight pool. Gold (#D4AF37 / #FFD77A) accents only. Never beige, never bright pastel, never flat cartoon.
- **Headline (爆款钩子)**: every image carries ONE huge bold Chinese headline (4–10 字) in elegant gold-foil serif or heavy sans, with subtle metallic gradient and thin gold underline, top or bottom third — a hook/number, not a description. Examples: "奢享时刻", "顶级配置", "高级感拉满".
- **Callouts**: 6–12 short punchy labels (1–6 字 each) in thin gold-bordered chips / fine-line tags near key objects, with eye-catching numbers/units. Big enough to read on a phone.
- **Focal hierarchy**: one spotlit hero object dominates against black; gold callouts read as refined editorial labels.
- **Consistency**: every page feels from the same luxury campaign — same black-gold palette, same marble accents, same spotlight, same elegant headline treatment.

Append the following suffix to **every** ImageGen prompt:
> `, black-gold luxury high-fashion vertical 9:16 poster illustration, deep matte black backdrop (#0B0B0B) with subtle marble veining, gleaming gold-foil metallic accents (#D4AF37 #FFD77A), fine gold linework, soft warm spotlight on a single hero object, premium editorial magazine elegance, elegant gold-foil Chinese headline with metallic gradient and thin gold underline, fine gold-bordered callout chips, ONE huge bold Chinese headline plus punchy numeric callouts, mobile-first vertical composition`

Negative cues (if model supports):
> `flat isometric diagram, beige background, bright pastel, flat cartoon, photoreal snapshot, tiny cramped text, horizontal 16:9, dense paragraphs, watermarks, neon cyberpunk, pixel art, hand-drawn doodle, grainy riso`
